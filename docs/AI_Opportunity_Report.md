## 3. Deep-Dive Opportunity: Why TenantEase AI Is the Ideal Strategic Bet

### 3.1 Alignment with Cynbit's Engineering Stack and Intern Mandate
Cynbit's [AI/ML Engineer Intern job specification](https://www.cynbit.in/careers/ai-ml-engineer-intern) outlines four core responsibilities:
1. **Develop document extraction and processing pipelines**: Property managers onboard dozens of multi-page PDF lease agreements and KYC documents (`Aadhaar`, `PAN`, `Passport`) every month. Automating structured extraction into `PostgreSQL`/`Supabase` directly eliminates manual data entry.
2. **Build and deploy AI chatbots and virtual assistants**: Tenants and landlords repeatedly ask clause-specific questions (*"What is my lock-in period?"*, *"Who pays for geyser repair?"*, *"What is the daily late fee after the grace period?"*). A citation-grounded Lease RAG assistant resolves 80%+ of queries instantaneously.
3. **Optimize prompt engineering for various LLM models**: Legal and financial property workflows require zero hallucination, strict JSON/Pydantic schema adherence, and resilience against adversarial prompt injection.
4. **Research and integrate emerging AI/ML technologies**: Replacing rigid calendar reminders with calibrated machine-learning delinquency risk scoring and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) agentic maintenance triage brings Cynbit's philosophy from "[How AI Agents Are Transforming Business Operations in 2026](https://www.cynbit.in/blog/ai-agents-business-operations)" directly into TenantEase.
